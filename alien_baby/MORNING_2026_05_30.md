## How to read the results (what we're testing)

The generalization hypothesis (your idea): the proprio "general approach" response
is the PRIMARY thing learned and generalizes broadly; VISION is a late refinement
recruited ONLY where the general class under-determines the action.

Distinguish two kinds of "same results":
- INVARIANCE (size; later color/shape/texture): response should be ~UNCHANGED.
  Read `both|cl` flat across the sweep.
- EQUIVARIANCE (distance, speed): response should change LAWFULLY.
  Distance: `mean_contact_step` should RISE ~linearly with ball-y and extrapolate
  past the trained range (~0.27-0.43). The battery fits `steps ~= slope*dist+b`
  and reports r. A positive, well-correlated slope = genuine distance generalization
  with a real internal time-prediction (the "farther = longer to get there" claim
  rendered as a number).

Key columns (all reach-conditional so unreachable placements don't masquerade as
generalization failures):
- `got_cl` = fraction that got a hand within striking range
- `both|cl` = touch rate AMONG episodes that got close (skill, not reach)
- `abl_L2` = vision ablation per bin. Hypothesis predicts abl RISES with eccentricity
  / appears where proprio can't resolve direction. (proprio runs show nan — expected.)

Decision tree:
- R43 abl_L2 ~flat-zero across bins  -> vision still not recruited even where needed
  (informative AGAINST the strong form; proprio generalization carries the task).
- R43 abl_L2 rises at the margin AND R43 both|cl > R44 at high eccentricity
  -> vision earns its keep exactly where the idea predicts. Strongest result.
- Both R43/R44 collapse (low got_cl everywhere) -> box 0.08 still too hard; narrow to
  0.05 and rerun (re-edit launch_phase_v.sh, relaunch).
